# Participant task sheet

Use participant codes, not names or emails, in the study data. Explain voluntary participation, withdrawal,
how observations are stored and who can access them. Obtain consent before recording. Use a dedicated test
account and synthetic postings. Do not collect passwords. The participant should know this is an educational
prototype and applications do not reach an employer.

1. Register and verify an account, then sign in. If mail delivery is unavailable, record the failure and use
   a pre-provisioned test account; do not count registration as successfully completed.
2. Enter academic information, three technical skills, two soft skills, career interests and any relevant
   certifications. Save, reload and find the saved values.
3. Find a recommended role. Explain in your own words what its score means and why it appears.
4. Open skill analysis. Identify one matched skill and one missing required/preferred skill, then find the
   corresponding improvement guidance. If no gaps exist, choose another synthetic role.
5. Search using a keyword and combine industry/type/location/required-skill filters. Clear them and recover
   from a no-results search.
6. Submit an expression of interest to a synthetic role, attempt a duplicate, sign out and revisit a protected page.

Facilitator: record completion, time, assistance, errors and comments in `evaluation/study/task-observations.csv`.
Avoid coaching unless the participant cannot continue; record all assistance. Give `sus-questionnaire.md`
immediately after tasks, without suggesting desired scores. These are tasks to execute, not completed results.
